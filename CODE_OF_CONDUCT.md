# Code of conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
The full text of version 2.1 at the link above applies in full; the summary below is for convenience.

## In short

We want a friendly, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent; give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Fuber AI and for the community.
- Not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
  publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behaviour through GitHub: open an issue at <https://github.com/fberkehan/orion/issues> (for a sensitive case, the
issue can simply ask a maintainer to contact you, without details), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull
request concerned. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions, and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban participants, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary
ban, permanent ban).
